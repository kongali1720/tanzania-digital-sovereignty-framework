## 1. Cybersecurity, Cryptography & API Standards

* **NIST SP 800-207 (Zero Trust Architecture):** Architectural principles for eliminating implicit network trust, enforcing dynamic policy evaluation (`Policy Engine` / `Policy Administrator`), and protecting resources across enterprise/sovereign boundaries.
* **ISO/IEC 27001 & ISO/IEC 27701:** Information security management system (ISMS) controls and privacy information management extensions for structured risk, access, and data protection governance.
* **FIPS 140-3 & PKI/Cryptographic Lifecycle Standards:** Requirements and operational practices for cryptographic modules/HSMs, certificate hierarchies, key rotation, and revocation (`CRL` / `OCSP`).
* **Web, API & Identity Protocols:** **TLS 1.3** (transport security), **Mutual TLS (mTLS)** (strong service/client authentication), **OAuth 2.0 / OIDC** (delegated authorization & identity federation), and **RFC 7807** (`application/problem+json` error structure).

---

## 2. Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) & Ledger Design Principles

* **Modular DPI & Interoperability:** Separation of core building blocks (identity/authentication, secure data exchange, consent/audit, and domain-owned registries) to avoid monolithic coupling and vendor lock-in.
* **Off-Chain Privacy & On-Chain Integrity:** Keeping sensitive personal or institutional records in access-controlled off-chain stores while anchoring canonicalized cryptographic hashes and audit events on permissioned ledgers where appropriate.

## 3. Comparative Research Areas: Tanzania & Indonesia

This framework uses comparative study of Tanzania and Indonesia strictly for **technical, architectural, and policy analysis**—not as a claim of official bilateral endorsement or active government mandate:

* **Tanzania Digital & Data Context:** Review official public-sector digital transformation policies, data protection and cybersecurity legal instruments, national identity/registry initiatives, and institutional mandates from authoritative Tanzanian sources before mapping any operational requirement.
* **Indonesia Digital & Governance Context:** Review official Indonesian digital government/SPBE architecture concepts, personal data protection rules, cybersecurity standards, and DPI/interoperability lessons from authoritative Indonesian sources as comparative reference material.

## 4. Source Verification & Citation Rules

When adding new facts, statistics, laws, or institutional references to this repository or Wiki:

1. **Prefer Primary & Authoritative Sources:** Cite official standards bodies, statutory texts, peer-reviewed research, or primary documentation rather than secondary summaries alone.
2. **Record Context & Date:** Note the document title, issuing body, publication/verification date, and applicable scope.
3. **Separate Evidence from Proposal:** Explicitly label whether a statement is a **verified fact**, a **proposed architecture/model**, an **assumption**, or a **recommendation**.
4. **Never Imply Unverified Partnerships:** Do not describe exploratory research or bilateral technical comparisons as formal agreements, funding commitments, or official endorsements.

> **Governance Disclaimer:** Reference entries in this document guide research and architectural alignment. All legal, regulatory, and institutional claims must be independently verified against primary sources prior to formal adoption.

# Executive Summary: National Digital Sovereignty, Blockchain & Cybersecurity Proposal

**Target Stakeholders:** Office of the President of the United Republic of Tanzania, Ministry of Information, Communication and Information Technology, and Bank of Tanzania  
**Scope:** Sovereign Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI), Permissioned Blockchain, Zero-Trust Cybersecurity, and Bilateral IT Partnership

## 1. Strategic Context
Tanzania has established core digital building blocks, including the National ICT Broadband Backbone (NICTBB), the National Internet Data Center (NIDC), the National Identification Authority (NIDA), and widespread mobile money adoption. To convert these foundations into high-trust national digital infrastructure, Tanzania must address:

* Fragmented data exchange across ministries and public registries.
* Vulnerability to document forgery, asset disputes, and supply-chain or royalty leakage.
* Growing cyber threats targeting critical public, financial, and logistics systems.

## 2. Core Pillars
1. **Interoperable E-Government & DPI:** Standardize data exchange across national identity, tax, business licensing, land, and health systems while keeping sovereign data hosted locally.
2. **Permissioned Blockchain for High-Trust Operations:** Deploy enterprise permissioned ledgers for land registries, mineral and agricultural export traceability, public procurement auditing, and verifiable credentials.
3. **Zero-Trust National Cybersecurity:** Enforce continuous identity verification, network micro-segmentation, national PKI, and integrated SOC/incident response across critical state infrastructure.
4. **Sustainable Partnership & Local Capacity:** Deliver through Joint Ventures, Build-Operate-Transfer (BOT), or revenue-sharing Public-Private Partnerships (PPP) paired with structured local engineering and cybersecurity training.

## 3. Phased Rollout (36 Months)
* **Phase 1 (Months 1–9):** Baseline IT and cybersecurity audit, interoperability standards, national PKI/Zero-Trust foundation, and regulatory sandbox alignment.
* **Phase 2 (Months 10–21):** Targeted pilots (such as mineral/export traceability and municipal land registry) integrated with identity verification and continuous security monitoring.
* **Phase 3 (Months 22–36):** Nationwide expansion of validated systems and regional East African digital trade/verification interoperability.

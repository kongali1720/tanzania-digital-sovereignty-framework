# Project Governance

## 1. Purpose

This document describes the proposed governance approach for the
Tanzania Digital Sovereignty Framework repository.

It does not establish a government institution, legal entity, public authority,
or official bilateral agreement.

## 2. Maintainer Responsibilities

Repository maintainers are responsible for:

- Reviewing issues and proposed changes.
- Maintaining documentation quality and consistency.
- Managing repository structure and releases.
- Applying the Code of Conduct.
- Reviewing security reports through appropriate private channels.

## 3. Decision-Making

Routine documentation changes may be reviewed and accepted by maintainers.

Substantial changes to project direction, architecture, policy positions,
or governance proposals should be discussed transparently through issues
or pull requests.

Maintainers may request revisions, additional evidence, or expert review.

## 4. Contributions

Community members may propose improvements through issues and pull requests.
All contributions are subject to review and the repository's contribution rules.

## 5. Transparency

The project should distinguish clearly between:

- Existing and verified capabilities.
- Research findings.
- Proposed architecture.
- Recommendations.
- Potential partnerships.
- Confirmed agreements.

No partnership, endorsement, government authorization, or institutional
commitment should be implied without verifiable evidence.

## 6. Changes to Governance

This document may be revised through a documented pull request.
Material changes should include an explanation of their rationale.
